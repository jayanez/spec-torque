# Tools for lexical and bilingual requirements research

Research snapshot: 2026-11-23. This is a source review, not a benchmark or an endorsement for product integration. No tool was installed, no model was downloaded and no excluded corpus or calibration fixture was opened for this investigation. #58's original scope and its admission gate remain unchanged.

## Ranked candidates

| Candidate | Concrete use | Constraints and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPU exact hash and character/token n-gram similarity | Group exact copies and rank near-copy candidates among authorized DEV documents; retain document IDs, revisions and original paths. | Most proportional for the current hundreds of documents. Similarity is not proof of shared ancestry or independence. Review clusters and uncertain pairs; do not discard text automatically. |
| [spaCy Matcher](https://spacy.io/usage/rule-based-matching) | Offline exploratory rules over tokens, lemmas, morphology and dependency labels; produce EN/ES candidate passages with offsets for human review. | Python runtime, pipeline artifacts, model licenses, versions, reproducibility and news/web versus requirements domain shift need checking. The [Spanish](https://spacy.io/models/es) and [English](https://spacy.io/models/en) small pipelines are trained on different genres. A rule can be deterministic while its supplied linguistic annotations have model error. Do not replace the shared Node/browser engine on this evidence. |
| [Microsoft Recognizers-Text](https://github.com/microsoft/Recognizers-Text) | Examine recognition of numbers, dates and units in EN/ES, including written quantities, for later measurement-related work such as E1.2. | JavaScript/TypeScript packages are listed, with .NET the primary implementation. Verify actual package versions, ES support by entity, offline behavior, licenses and browser bundling before a trial. It is not a general failure-semantics, paraphrase or completeness detector and does not directly resolve P2.2/P1.4. |
| [Microsoft Presidio](https://microsoft.github.io/presidio/) | Optional local sensitive-data preflight if future authorized document custody calls for it; custom recognizers combine patterns and context. | Designed for PII; the [language guide](https://github.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/blob/main/docs/analyzer/languages.md) says default models and recognizers are English. Python and configured models/recognizers add costs; no guarantee that all sensitive content is detected. Not recommended as the requirements detector. |
| [NVIDIA NeMo Curator](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/curator/latest/curate-text/process-data/deduplication) | At much larger scale, generate exact, MinHash/LSH and semantic near-duplicate candidates. | Current documented workflows require GPU; semantic mode also runs embeddings. GPU access, stack, costs and model suitability for both languages are unverified. Too much infrastructure for this bounded DEV inventory; useful future option, not a lineage oracle. |
| [Transformers.js](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js/index) with a separately chosen multilingual embedding model | Optional local semantic retrieval to rank possible paraphrases for review, including across EN/ES. | Requires a specific model, artifact/license and download policy; semantic similarity does not establish provenance or equivalence. Validate browser/Node runtime, memory, latency and offline behavior. No implicit model downloads or cloud calls. This is a separate pre-registered experiment, not part of #58's product behavior. |
| [Azure custom text classification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/language-service/custom-text-classification-data-privacy-security) | Possible separately authorized cloud research on appropriately governed material. | Classification sends text to a service and training uses uploaded data; incompatible with automatic local-first analysis. Do not submit project/private material or create resources under this spike. |

## Research evidence and transfer limits

- [Huertas and Juárez-Ramírez (2013)](https://consensus.app/papers/huertas-juárez%E2%80%90ram%C3%ADrez-towards-assessing-the-quality-of-functional/w:015dca88-723c-4d36-a8d5-ce07ec83c006/?utm_source=chatgpt) describe controlled EN/ES requirements and a context-free grammar. Only the abstract was available through Consensus; the controlled-language setup does not establish performance on freely written project specifications.
- [Veizaga, Shin and Briand (2024)](https://consensus.app/papers/veizaga-shin-automated-smell-detection-and-recommendation-in/w:92669d84-e68c-4fe0-bf52-cbf27bd92326/?utm_source=chatgpt) report a requirements-smell tool evaluated on 2,725 annotated requirements across 13 systems in one financial-domain case study. Their abstract reports 89% precision and recall for smell detection. These figures do not establish EN/ES parity, performance on #58 practices, independence of lineages or transportability to SpecTorque.
- [Silcock et al. (2022), preprint](https://consensus.app/papers/silcock-d%E2%80%99amicowong-noiserobust-deduplication-at-scale/w:350f5671-56bb-41ba-847b-44bfb64ba8cc/?utm_source=chatgpt) evaluate near-duplicate retrieval on historical news and report gains from neural approaches over their hashing/n-gram baselines. The domain and task differ; similarity does not itself prove shared origin. The abstract alone does not establish an improvement on requirements in EN/ES.

## Proposed bounded next step

For #58, finish enumerating the authorized DEV revisions and create a local, reproducible candidate index: provenance and paths, exact byte hashes, normalized-text hashes and ranked n-gram similarities. Preserve original bytes and per-document evidence, capture retrieval failures and the search denominator, and manually adjudicate uncertain reuse. Use a fixed, documented sampling and ranking method and examine negatives; same-topic wording alone must not collapse lineages. Screen the actual practice result and source context before counting the same concrete defect across two independent repositories and two lineages. The synthetic controls are development aids, not independent natural validation.

For a possible future tool comparison, pre-register a narrow question, version and artifact hashes, language-specific negative controls, resource measurements, abstentions and independent lineage-separated labels; compare the unchanged 0.1.4 engine and candidate methods on the same data. Keep DEV iterations within the existing stop rules and reserve a fresh single-use test set. Any product proposal requires the historical 716-prediction replay, the specified precision gates and host-independent packaging. H2c remains stopped and T2/T2b and #57/#60 calibration material remain excluded. No model-based candidate is authorized by this literature review.

## Bounded local check

On the 223 documents already fetched from the authorized DEV manifest, `python3 scripts/spike-58/hash-dev.py` found zero whole-document duplicate groups by byte SHA-256 and zero after UTF-8 decoding with replacement, NFC normalization, case-folding and whitespace collapse. This is a measurement of that partial retrieval only: it does not detect copied passages, paraphrases, translations, other revisions or repositories omitted by the 100-document cap. Zero matches do not establish independent lineages. The command emits aggregate counts and does not modify the input documents.
