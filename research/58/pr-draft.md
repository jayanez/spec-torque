# Spike: inventory bilingual lexical signals

## Problem and result

The lexical practices can change status when equivalent EN/ES wording changes, and can count a keyword without the intended information. This spike adds reproducible synthetic controls and records each observation, the current decision and the limits of a bounded natural-source search. No detector correction meets the two-repository, two-lineage admission criterion on the evidence collected; recurrence remains pending.

The engine, rubric, scoring, UI and integrations are unchanged. H2c remains stopped. The historical comparator and base have identical detector source, but a future correction still needs its own 716-prediction replay and independent precision evaluation.

## Validation and limitations

- Node 22.20.0; `npm ci`, `npm run check` (154 tests) and `npm run package:integrations` passed.
- The broad `npm run check` loaded clarification-study fixtures through `tests/clarify.test.ts`; their relationship to excluded #57/#60 calibration material has not been verified. No findings from those fixtures are used in the spike.
- Synthetic controls are constructed, not independent validation. The authorized DEV-source search was bounded and incomplete; no absence claim is made.
- Independent Luna Latest review of the final change remains required before merge, with access limited to #58 material; do not proceed if calibration material cannot be isolated.
